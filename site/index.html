What is Heartopia?

Heartopia is a relaxing slow-life simulation game from XD. The official site presents it as a calm social and creative life-sim experience with fashion, in-game footage, and multi-language support. Fan sites commonly track PC, iOS, Android, and Steam routes, plus codes, hobbies, pets, house planning, and wiki databases.

FAQ

Is this an official Heartopia website?

No. Heartopia Hub is an independent fan resource. It is not affiliated with XD, the developer, publisher, or official Heartopia channels.

Are all codes guaranteed active today?

No. Codes are pulled from public fan trackers and can expire without notice. The useful part is the priority, expiry note, reward category, and source date so players know what to test first.

Why include database rows if the full game data is still changing?

Because a good guide site should be transparent about sample size. This launch version gives verified-style tables and clear update paths, then can expand into hundreds of item, fish, recipe, NPC, and pet rows later.
