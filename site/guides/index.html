Heartopia Beginner Guide

A practical route for your first sessions: unlock core hobbies, collect free rewards, avoid wasting rare materials, and build a daily loop that keeps progress moving.

First 10 Steps

  1. Claim mailbox rewards and test current redeem codes before spending premium items.
  2. Follow main quests until the watch, map, home plot, and first tools are introduced.
  3. Unlock Gardening with Blanc and keep all starter plant boxes active.
  4. Unlock Fishing with Vanya and learn line tension: white is safe, yellow is caution, red means release.
  5. Start a Potato or Tomato route depending on session length.
  6. Record NPC locations and pick one friendship target for gift testing.
  7. Use bus stops and the map icon tracker instead of wandering without a goal.
  8. Save rare timber, flawless fluorite, perfumes, and event flowers until upgrade needs are clear.
  9. Push D.G. progress toward Cooking and Birdwatching unlocks.
  10. End each session by planting long-cycle crops and checking event timers.

Daily Route

  • Mailbox and codes
  • Quest objective
  • Target NPC talk
  • Crop harvest and replant
  • One fish or animal route

Hobby Unlock Notes

  • Gardening: early tutorial route with Blanc, starter plant boxes, and watering loop.
  • Fishing: early route with Vanya, repair kit step, and fishing rod unlock.
  • Cooking: commonly tied to D.G. 5-6 and Massimo's cafe route.
  • Birdwatching: associated with Bailey J and Pet Store upstairs route.
  • Pet Care: commonly tied to D.G. 12 and Mrs. Joan's Pet Shop route.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Do not spend rare materials before upgrade and gift needs are clear.
  • Do not treat unknown-expiry codes as permanent.
  • Do not plant only long-cycle crops during short active sessions.
  • Do not mix pet-shop cats/dogs, Oak-Oak, and wild animal troughs into one route.
  • Do not over-decorate before room jobs and walking paths are planned.
