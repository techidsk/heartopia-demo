Quick Download Answer

Fastest safe option: use an official PC or mobile build from the official Heartopia/XD channels or trusted app stores. Avoid random APK mirrors and unofficial repacks, especially if a page asks for account credentials or separate patch files.

Before changing devices or launchers, verify account linking and save behavior in the official client or official support docs.

Official Download Paths

  • iOS: use App Store when available in your region.
  • Android: use Google Play or official publisher flow.
  • PC: use official PC route, Steam route, or publisher launcher only.
  • Publisher site: use the Heartopia/XD language and platform selector, not copied pages.

Reported Requirements

  • iOS: commonly reported as iOS 12.0 or later.
  • Android: commonly reported as Android 7.0+.
  • PC: DirectX 11 and broadband connection are common requirements.
  • Storage: keep at least 10 GB free for patches and cache.

Safety Rules

  • Do not download APKs from random mirrors.
  • Do not enter game login on a third-party download page.
  • Do not install patcher executables outside official channels.
  • Check region availability before assuming a store listing is missing.
