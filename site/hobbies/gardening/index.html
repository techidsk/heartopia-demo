Quick Gardening Answer

Plant short-cycle crops while actively playing and longer crops before logging off. Buy seeds from Blanc, keep starter boxes full, and harvest often because active farming is the core proficiency loop.

Fast session: Tomato. One-hour session: Potato. Offline block: Wheat or Lettuce.

Blanc Schedule Notes

  • Garden Shop in Town Center during normal daytime windows.
  • Town Square around midday.
  • Home garden or inside house in early morning and evening.
  • Use map tracking when his icon is available.

Level Fast

  • Harvest crops regularly for the main XP source.
  • Plant and water instead of leaving empty boxes.
  • Clear weeds when they appear for bonus proficiency.
  • Match crop time to your session length.
