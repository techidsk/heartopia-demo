Do

  • Cook after gathering fruit, fish, or vegetables.
  • Use Recipe Finder before spending a rare ingredient stack.
  • Watch for the spatula QTE timer before cooking event materials.

Do Not

  • Burn dishes by ignoring QTEs.
  • Spend limited event ingredients before recipe value is known.
  • Sell raw materials without checking cooked value first.
