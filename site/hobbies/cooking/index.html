Quick Cooking Answer

Rush cooking after the first two hobbies because it combines fish, crops, and fruit into higher-value dishes. Do not spam recipes blindly: missing the spatula QTE can turn output into lower-value Bizarre Food.

Best early pattern: grow fruit or Potato, fish nearby, then convert extras into jams or Fish and Chips.

Do

  • Cook after gathering fruit, fish, or vegetables.
  • Watch for the spatula QTE timer.
  • Use cooking as a Gold route after gardening and fishing are steady.

Do Not

  • Burn dishes by ignoring QTEs.
  • Spend rare ingredients before recipe value is known.
  • Sell raw materials without checking cooked value first.
