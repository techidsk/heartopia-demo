Quick Fishing Answer

Unlock fishing through Vanya during the early tutorial, then use water shadows and line-tension color to land catches. If the weather turns Rainbow, prioritize rare-fish routes such as Giantwood River and Starlight Sea before routine lake farming.

Line tension rule: white means reel, yellow means slow down, red means release before the line snaps.

Rare Fish Priority

  • Rainbow weather: route Giantwood River and Starlight Sea first.
  • High level: Swordfish and Bluefin Tuna require late fishing progress.
  • Hidden timing: Secret Pond Koi is a midnight route.

Fishing Tips

  • Use Mermaid Fish Attractor when the code pool gives it to you.
  • Track missing fish in a list by water type, time, and weather.
  • Do not waste premium bait on routine early catches.
