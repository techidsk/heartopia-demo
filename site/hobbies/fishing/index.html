Rare Fish Priority

  • Rainbow weather: route Giantwood River and Starlight Sea first.
  • High level: Swordfish, Bluefin Tuna, and Smooth Hammerhead require late progress.
  • Hidden timing: Secret Pond Koi is a midnight route.

Fishing Tips

  • Use Mermaid Fish Attractor only after the routine route is clear.
  • Track missing fish by water type, time, weather, and level gate.
  • Jump from this page to Fish Tracker when a target needs multiple sessions.
