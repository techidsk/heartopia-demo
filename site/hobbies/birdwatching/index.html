Birdwatching Conditions

  • Tool: camera or binocular route before serious collecting.
  • Weather: sunny, clear, rain, and special windows should be tracked separately.
  • Habitat: town, field, forest, coast, lake edge, and mountain routes should not be mixed.
  • State: sighted, photographed, and album-confirmed are different completion states.

Goodcase Checklist

  • Search and filter routes before walking the map.
  • Save local tasks for missing weather or rare targets.
  • Use Map and Events instead of rerouting blindly.
  • Pair daily bird checks with NPC and shop routes.
