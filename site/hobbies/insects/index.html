Insect Route Conditions

  • Tool: Bubble Net route before serious collecting.
  • Time: day, evening, and night windows should be separated.
  • Weather: rain and special weather can change the priority route.
  • Habitat: flowers, trees, water edges, mountain routes, and town plants need separate loops.

Goodcase Checklist

  • Search and filter routes before walking every region.
  • Use Timer for time-window targets.
  • Use Map and Events for habitat and weather handoffs.
  • Do not sell a first catch before collection or request needs are clear.
