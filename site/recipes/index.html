Early Gold

Potato plus routine fish is the cleanest early cooking loop to test before rare recipes.

Low Risk

Jam and mushroom dishes use repeatable inputs, so they are safer for QTE practice.

High Risk

Do not cook event materials or rare fish until the recipe value is known.
