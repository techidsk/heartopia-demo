Quick Dog Food Answer

For adoptable dogs, Dog Food from Mrs. Joan is the safest baseline feed. Community trackers still disagree on one universal favorite for every dog, so test low-cost foods in your own file before treating a breed reaction as a global rule.

Keep cats/dogs, Oak-Oak, and wild animal troughs as three separate routes.

Pet Troubleshooting

  • If a food gives weak reaction, switch back to Dog Food.
  • If a route is blocked, recheck D.G. level and pet-shop onboarding.
  • If Oak-Oak is missing, look for the forest quest line rather than Mrs. Joan.
