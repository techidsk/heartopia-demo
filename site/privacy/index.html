Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Heartopia Hub collects, how advertising may work, and how visitors can control cookies and personalized ads.

Information We Collect

This static website does not require an account and does not ask for passwords, game account data, payment details, or personal profile information. If you contact the site owner by email, your message and email address may be used to respond to your request.

Cookies And Analytics

The current version does not include analytics scripts. If analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to name the provider, explain the data collected, and describe opt-out choices.

Google AdSense And Advertising

When Google AdSense or other advertising partners are enabled, third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

Visitors can review ad personalization choices through Google's advertising settings and can learn more about third-party advertising choices through recognized industry opt-out resources. If this site serves users in regions with additional consent requirements, a compliant consent mechanism should be added before personalized advertising is enabled.

External Links

This site may link to official game pages, publisher channels, and community resources. External websites have their own privacy practices and are not controlled by Heartopia Hub.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page. Before AdSense submission, replace the placeholder email with a monitored inbox.
